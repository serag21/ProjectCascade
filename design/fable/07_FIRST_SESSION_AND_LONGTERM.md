# 07 — First session and long-term enjoyment

Timings are targets to measure with the existing funnel metrics (`first_action`, `first_catch`, first full round…), not claims.

## 1. First session

### 0–10 seconds
- Spawn at the Meadow gate facing north. No menu, no text wall. The camera pulls back for 1.2 s.
- In view: the Clover Tree on the skyline, a bank ring 8 studs ahead, three Pips 20 studs ahead on the lawn. The player's ribbon is already trailing behind them.
- A dotted **ghost ring** is drawn on the ground around the Pips with a small running-figure icon tracing it (first round only). Hint line: "Run a circle around the Sparks".
- These three starter Pips are slowed (existing starter allowance) and cannot be taken by others.
- **Target:** first input feedback ≤ 2 s (the ribbon), first loop closed ≤ 10 s.

### 10–30 seconds
- First cinch: three pops on a rising scale, payout tag, Pips join the parade.
- The south bank ring pulses; a ground chevron points to it. Running through banks three: fountain, "+glow".
- A Cubloom flock and the stone pens are visible to the west; a second Pip pod in the tall grass.
- Hint (once): "Bigger ring, bigger score".
- **Target:** first bank ≤ 30 s.

### 30 seconds – 2 minutes (first round)
- 25–30 s: a bloom bud swells and chimes; at 30 s it opens — first eight-creature opportunity.
- First dash prompt appears only when a Bunvolt first hops out of a loop: "Dash to snap a ring shut" (once).
- 49 s: rumble, route chevrons, horn. 55 s: the first Stampede. Whatever the player manages, the column is spectacular and at least a few are catchable.
- 85 s: the Golden Runaway beam. 110 s: buzzer, auto-bank.
- Results sheet: score, medal, the **Best Loop card**, one named next goal with a CLAIM button, 10 s to the next round.
- **Target:** round completed by 2:00 with ≥ 4 loops closed and ≥ 1 loop of 3+.

### 2–10 minutes (rounds 2–5)
| Round | New thing | How it is introduced |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | Flutterwink perch; pinch walls | Perch glows; hint once when a ribbon end first touches a pen wall: "Walls can finish your ring" |
| 2 | First upgrade affordable | Results sheet offers one (Dash Boots) |
| 3 | **First Legend** (guaranteed), Static Puff | Sky dims, horn; the Puff arrives with "Dash through it" |
| 3 | Fizz Canyon unlock visible | Progress bar on the results sheet (existing unlock threshold) |
| 4–5 | Chain multiplier awareness | The chain bar appears only once the player has reached x2 for the first time |

No hint repeats after it has been acted on, and none appears after round 3.

### 10 minutes – first hour
- Fizz Canyon unlocks (existing `unlock=30` banked; revisit in WP-14): a different way to play, not a recolour.
- Signature stars begin to appear in the Album with each species' counterplay tip unlocked after the first catch.
- First perk slot (rank 2) and Heat after a Silver medal (existing).
- Daily Stampede is offered on the results sheet after round 5 (never before).
- Aurora Arcade is a visible goal for session two (existing `unlock=90`).
- **End-of-session hook:** the results sheet's "next goal" plus the records board line "Legend in N rounds".

### What the first hour must not contain
Loading lectures, forced tutorials, a locked camera, more than one new idea per round, any purchase prompt, any penalty for leaving.

## 2. Long-term enjoyment

### Mastery ladder
| Tier | Skill | Evidence the game shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Close loops reliably | First Bronze |
| 2 | Size loops to the pod; bank at the right ring | ROUNDUP tags, bank multiplier ×1.5 |
| 3 | SNAP and TIGHT on demand; beat each species' escape | Signature stars (existing 10/25/50) |
| 4 | Herd to terrain; PINCH; MEDLEY | Technique counts on the results sheet |
| 5 | Stampede horseshoes; 8+ loops | STAMPEDE! tag; Best Loop card |
| 6 | Chain x4 through a whole round; flawless ribbon | Gold / Radiant medals |
| 7 | Heat 3–5; Daily Stampede top placements | Heat medals, daily board |

Skill, not stats, moves a player up this ladder: the full upgrade set changes dash recharge by 0.9 s, snap radius by 3 studs and capacity by 9.

### Evolving challenge without new systems
- **Heat 0–5** (existing), re-keyed to loops: shorter ribbon, shorter chain window, more Static Puffs.
- **Weekly Remix** (existing), re-themed: *Long Ribbon Week* (+30 % ribbon, −20 % score), *Double Stampede*, *Golden Rush*, *Shy Week* (awareness +4), *Starfall Everywhere*, *Big Herd*.
- **Daily Stampede** (existing): seeded pods and one seeded stampede on the Trial paddock; same for everyone; best-of-day board.
- **Per-arena difficulty:** Meadow is forgiving; Canyon demands prediction; Aurora demands control. Medals per arena already exist.

### Rare and memorable
Golden Runaway every round; Legends on a visible schedule; Legend trails earned with three tokens each; Gilded album; FULL BLOOM, ROYAL COURT and STAMPEDE! as named feats.

### Rewards that mean something
- Cosmetics that change the **ribbon itself** (colour, texture, cinch effect, pop sound set) — the most visible thing a player owns. Existing trail cosmetics map onto ribbon styles.
- Titles and parade formations (existing).
- No reward increases power beyond the three small upgrades. Nothing is sold that affects score.

### Social motivation
Shared Legends and Goldens; assists; arena-wide STAMPEDE! call-outs; positive-only podium; records boards; the Best Loop card as a screenshot.

### Content cadence after launch (possibilities, not commitments)
Each is a data-only or near data-only addition under this design:
1. A fourth species per arena (new behaviour state, new habitat).
2. A fourth arena with a new movement rule (e.g. bounce-pad cloud tops; conveyor tides).
3. New Legends on the existing wrap system.
4. Remix rules.
5. Ribbon cosmetic sets.

### Grind guards
No daily chores or streaks; goals are play-shaped and rerollable (existing); the album is 12 + 12 gilded + 3 legends; signature stars cap at 50 catches; nothing requires more than normal play at a player's own skill.
