# DECISIONS — SPARK STAMPEDE

Append-only design/engineering decision log. Newest last. Earlier decisions live in the charter, research and progress documents; this file starts with the 2026-11-04 cloud session.

| # | Date | Decision | Rationale | Revisit when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D1 | 2026-11-04 | Merge `wip/spark-interrupted-20261103` (biome arena events, PB split checkpoints) after review; complete its interrupted supervisor change. | Useful, coherent, tested; avoids losing owner-session work. | — |
| D2 | 2026-11-04 | Deepen skill with dash-through multi-catch, personal statics, Spark Gate herding, Stampede Run lanes, golden finale, scramble-on-hit and buzzer forfeiture rather than adding new buttons. | Keeps the 3-button control scheme (mobile friendly) while adding routing, prediction, timing and risk. | Playtests show confusion or frustration. |
| D3 | 2026-11-04 | Difficulty (Heat) and statics are **personal**, not arena-wide. | Shared creatures cannot be made harder for one player without hurting a newcomer in the same arena. | — |
| D4 | 2026-11-04 | Upgrades stay as modest permanent stats; new depth comes from perk side-grades with explicit downsides. | Owner requirement: upgrades must create choices, not just larger numbers. | Telemetry shows one dominant loadout. |
| D5 | 2026-11-04 | No prestige/rebirth at launch. Long tail is rank (50), heat medals (3 arenas × 6 heats), album tiers, achievements and daily/weekly goals. | Prestige resets suit long grind economies; this economy is short by design and resets would erase meaningful choices. | Post-launch data shows players exhausting rank. |
| D6 | 2026-11-04 | Daily/weekly goals with no streak, no loss on missed days, no paid skips. | Retention value without manipulative pressure (AGENTS hard constraint). | — |
| D7 | 2026-11-04 | Glow is never multiplied by heat; heat multiplies only skill score/XP. | Prevents grinding incentives and inflation. | — |
| D8 | 2026-11-04 | Third-party dev tools (luau-lsp, selene, lune, rojo binaries) were not executed: the session's permission policy blocked running downloaded binaries other than the official Luau CLI. Offline QA uses the official Luau runtime/compiler plus purpose-built engine doubles. | Respect permission boundary. | Owner allowlists these tools. |
| D9 | 2026-11-04 | Placeholder audio uses Roblox client built-in `rbxasset://sounds/*` files until the owner uploads the original WAVs. | No account/upload action is permitted; built-ins need no permissions. Exact availability must be verified in Studio. | Owner uploads assets. |
| D10 | 2026-11-05 | `FINAL_PRODUCTION_PLAN.md` is the executable blueprint; where it differs from `GAME1_DESIGN_SPEC.md`, the plan wins. | Final direction review; one source of truth for the production sprint. | Owner Checkpoint B/C findings. |
| D11 | 2026-11-05 | **Synchronized Stampede Cycles**: each arena shares a 120 s cycle (110 play + 10 intermission). Late joiners play a partial round (≥40 s left) or a warm-up; records/PB splits/Heat unlocks count only for full rounds. | Personal timers made the golden finale and buzzer incoherent and removed shared climaxes; shared rounds create social spectacle without lobby waits (max 10 s). | Studio shows the intermission feels like waiting. |
| D12 | 2026-11-05 | Intermission podium shows **positive awards only** (Top Parade, Longest Chain, Golden Catcher, Best Flanker, Static Buster); numeric session table stays opt-in; `showOnPodium` opt-out. | Recognition without shaming; kid-friendly social design. | — |
| D13 | 2026-11-05 | **Herd replication v2**: server keeps authoritative positions and stops moving models; it broadcasts compact per-arena buffers over an `UnreliableRemoteEvent` at 10 Hz; clients interpolate and animate creatures. | Anchored 10 Hz CFrame replication is steppy and bandwidth-heavy; living creatures are the core of the catch moment. | Studio shows desync between rendered and authoritative positions. |
| D14 | 2026-11-05 | Presentation is a **launch gate** (plan §11). Feel over features; feature freeze after Phase 8. | The biggest launch risk is a flat first impression, not missing systems. | — |
| D15 | 2026-11-05 | **Daily Stampede**: a seeded 75 s solo trial with normalized stats (upgrades/perks ignored), unlimited retries, first daily completion rewarded, XP for 5 runs/day. | Fair skill yardstick and a fun, non-chore daily return; seeded dailies are proven elsewhere (research M1). | Engagement data shows low trial uptake. |
| D16 | 2026-11-05 | **Public leaderboards cover Daily Stampede only.** Normal rounds keep personal records per arena×heat plus the per-server podium; arena records boards show today's Daily top 5. | Normal-round scores depend on server population, density scaling and heat stacking: unfair as public ranking. | — |
| D17 | 2026-11-05 | **AFK fix**: completion XP and round-count goals need ≥3 catches; two idle rounds auto-toggle Sit out. | Audit: `roundXp(0)=60` let idle players earn ~1,800 XP/h and finish "rounds" goals. | — |
| D18 | 2026-11-05 | **Staged threats for new players**: no sweeps/statics in round 1, sweeps from round 2, statics from round 3; a dash practice line after the first bank; coaching hints persist (`flags.seenHints`). | First-minute clarity; teach one thing at a time (research P3). | Human tests show boredom in round 1. |
| D19 | 2026-11-05 | **Progressive disclosure** of menu tabs and HUD elements, with a "!" badge only when something is new or claimable. | Seven tabs at minute one overwhelm new players. | — |
| D20 | 2026-11-05 | **Every rank-up rewards** something; add Parade Formations, Bank Bursts and Name Tags (cosmetic, visible to others); glow sink target ≥25 h for regular players. | Audit: 32 of 49 rank-ups granted nothing; economy ran out at ~14 h. | Telemetry on glow balances after launch. |
| D21 | 2026-11-05 | **Species Signatures**: each behaviour family has a signature technique (DASH, PRECISE, TOGETHER, RHYTHM) with bonus and album stars. | Makes the 12 species skill puzzles, not value numbers; connects collection to mastery (research C5). | Players ignore signatures (analytics `signature_catch`). |
| D22 | 2026-11-05 | **Aurora Starfall** replaces Aurora's Spark Gate; per-arena obstacle layouts become data-driven (`arena.obstacles`). | Two arenas shared one encounter and all three shared one layout. | — |
| D23 | 2026-11-05 | Dailies become 2 play goals + "Finish today's Daily Stampede"; one free reroll; claim from results; still no streaks or expiry pressure. | Passive checklists felt like chores; streaks cause FOMO complaints (research N3). | — |
| D24 | 2026-11-05 | **Weekly Remix** rotation of data-driven modifiers that never make the game harder for newcomers (launch-desirable). | Zero-content-cost freshness and update cadence (research C8). | — |
| D25 | 2026-11-05 | **Contested-catch assists** (+15 style within 1.5 s / 8 studs) and **player non-collision** via collision groups. | Turns "stealing" into teamwork; prevents body-blocking (research N5). | — |
| D26 | 2026-11-05 | **Profile v3 lands once in Phase 1** with every field the plan needs (cosmetics, signatures, trial, flags, new settings). | Avoids repeated migrations during the sprint. | — |
| D27 | 2026-11-05 | Rejected for launch: trading, eggs/pets with stats, PvP stealing/knockback, rebirth, hub lobby, AFK modes, energy timers, paid boosts/luck, crafting/bases, extra arenas before launch. | Bloat, griefing or policy risk; dilutes the active-skill identity (plan §16). | Post-launch data and owner direction. |
